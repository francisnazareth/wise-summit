        Platform considerations

        Required design considerations

        Security and governance

        Role-based permissions, audit trail, approval history, sensitivity-aware content handling, and clear decision rights.

        Bilingual operations

        Arabic and English workflows for briefs, website content, invitations, registration, leadership presentations, and protocol materials.

        Integration-ready

        Designed to connect with Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, registration platform, app, CRM, finance/procurement, and analytics sources.

        Leadership clarity

        Executive dashboards focused on readiness, risk, decisions required, approvals pending, and impact trajectory.

        Operational accountability

        Every task has an owner, deadline, dependency, status, source document, and escalation path.

        AI with guardrails

        Agents draft, summarize, recommend, and flag risks — but approvals remain with accountable human owners.

        Public-private separation

        Clear separation between internal operating data and public website/app content approved for external audiences.

        Post-summit continuity

        The platform does not end on summit day; it tracks outcomes, commitments, partner follow-up, and future edition maturity.
